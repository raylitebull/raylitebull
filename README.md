## 🚀 Flagship Project: [MacroPhase](https://github.com/MacroPhase/App)

**An adaptive nutrition platform that learns how your body actually works.**

[**macrophase.app**](https://macrophase.app) · [**GitHub**](https://github.com/MacroPhase/App)

MacroPhase goes far beyond calorie counting. It's a native Android nutrition and metabolic intelligence platform built around a simple idea: **your energy expenditure shouldn't be a static number calculated from a formula. It should evolve with real data.**

Designed and engineered from the ground up, MacroPhase combines adaptive mathematical modeling, a local-first data engine, AI-powered coaching, and a connected ecosystem of tools.

### 🧠 Adaptive Metabolic Intelligence
- **Dynamic TDEE estimation** powered by a custom state-space model using Kalman filtering, smoothing, and adaptive trend analysis.
- **Signal over noise:** models weight fluctuations, water retention, and energy balance to extract meaningful trends from messy real-world data.
- **Personalized strategy:** continuously evolving expenditure estimates, calorie targets, and body-composition insights informed by actual progress.

### ⚡ Native, Local-First Engineering
- **Offline by design.** Room/SQLite and reactive Kotlin flows keep the core experience fast, private, and functional without an internet connection.
- **Optional cloud synchronization** for a connected experience without sacrificing local ownership.
- **Jetpack Compose throughout**, with custom animations, refined interactions, interactive 3D nutritional visuals, and a carefully designed interface.
- **Flexible food logging** with structured nutritional databases, natural-language parsing, and streamlined everyday workflows.

### 🤖 AI-Native Ecosystem
- **Phase Coach:** an integrated AI nutrition assistant that can reason over your nutrition data, analyze progress, and help guide decisions.
- **MacroPhase Cloud:** connects the mobile experience with web services, synchronization, and AI integrations.
- **MCP + REST interfaces:** expose MacroPhase functionality to external AI agents, developer tools, and automation workflows.
- **MacroPhase CLI (`mp`):** manage food logs, search foods, inspect trends, and interact with your nutrition data directly from the terminal.
- **Phase Tasks:** scheduled reminders and AI-generated nutrition reviews that bring proactive intelligence into the experience.

### 🧪 Engineering Beyond the App
- **MacroBench:** a dedicated evaluation suite built with Inspect AI to benchmark language and vision models against realistic nutrition-agent tasks.
- Tests tool execution, food interpretation, structured logging, multimodal understanding, and database operations.
- Built to measure real-world agent reliability rather than just chatbot performance.

**Kotlin · Jetpack Compose · Room · Python · Ktor · PostgreSQL · MCP · Applied State-Space Modeling**
